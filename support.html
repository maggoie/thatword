Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to translatethatword@outlook.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Add the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose That Word.
  3. Also add Apple's keyboards for the languages you know. It helps That Word recognise words from them.

There's no Full Access switch to turn on, and you don't need one.

Use it

Type on your usual keyboard. When a word is missing, write it in a language you know, then hold or tap 🌐 to switch to That Word and tap a suggestion. Tap the magnifier to search again. Tap → language to change the language you're writing in, and ABC to type on That Word itself.

Common questions

No suggestions appear.

That Word needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer). Open the That Word app and check the Apple Intelligence section: it says whether it's ready, turned off or still downloading. If it's off, turn it on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. If the keyboard says Apple Intelligence doesn't support a language yet, that language can't be used for now.

It says “That's a whole sentence”.

That Word looks up words and short phrases, up to four words. It doesn't translate whole sentences.

Why doesn't it ask for Full Access?

Without Full Access, iOS blocks the keyboard from the internet. That's how we make sure nothing you type can leave your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.

How do I remove it?

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards, swipe left on That Word and tap Delete. Deleting the app removes it completely.

When reporting a bug

Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, the app you were typing in, the keyboard language, and the words you typed (only if you're comfortable sharing them).
