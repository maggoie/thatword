Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

The short version: That Word collects no data. What you type is never stored, never sent anywhere and never seen by us. The keyboard has no internet access.

What the keyboard reads, and why

When That Word is your active keyboard, it reads the text just before the cursor in the field you're typing in. That's how it finds the word you want to look up. This happens only in your iPhone's memory, only while the keyboard is open. The text is never written to storage and is discarded when you move on.

How suggestions are made

Suggestions are made on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence, using the on-device model only. That Word does not use Apple's Private Cloud Compute or any other server.

No internet access

That Word never asks for Full Access. Without it, iOS does not let a keyboard connect to the internet. The That Word app contains no networking code either.

What is stored on your iPhone

One setting: the language you chose for the keyboard. It stays on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No third-party code or services in the app.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we have none.

Information Apple may share with us

If you turned on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple collects these, not That Word, and they don't include what you type. We use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off in Settings at any time.

If you email us

If you write to translatethatword@outlook.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to any list, and we delete the conversation when it's no longer needed.

Children

That Word collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there's nothing to access, correct or delete, apart from any emails you've sent us. Ask us and we'll delete them. If you live in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We'll never change the app to collect what you type.

Who we are
