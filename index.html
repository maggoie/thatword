For the word you can't recall.

That Word is an offline iPhone keyboard. Write the word you're missing in a language you know, switch to That Word, and tap the right word in the language you're writing.

Coming soon to the App Store Privacy policy Support

Nothing you type leaves your iPhone.

That Word never asks for Full Access, so iOS blocks it from the internet. Suggestions come from Apple Intelligence, using the model on your iPhone only. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Requires an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer), turned on.

Words, not sentences

That Word looks up words and short phrases, up to four words. It's for the word you're missing, not for writing the sentence for you. A good way to pick up new vocabulary, too.
